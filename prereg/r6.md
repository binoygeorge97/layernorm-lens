# Pre-registration: R6, the TD-MPC2 encoder lens

Date: 24 September 2026. Author: Binoy George.
Identified by the annotated git tag `prereg-r6`. The author also creates a GitHub
Release from that tag, which records a server-side timestamp. This file is never
edited after tagging; any deviation goes in a separate, dated file,
`prereg/r6-deviations.md`.

## Question

Is the lens of TD-MPC2's first encoder layer (Linear → LayerNorm) sharp, and does it
sit where the states the robot actually visits are?

## Models

- Single-task 5M TD-MPC2 checkpoints for cartpole-swingup, cheetah-run, walker-run,
  humanoid-run and dog-run, as listed in `experiments/r6_tdmpc2/config.yaml` at the
  tagged commit.
- Seed 1 is the primary checkpoint for each task. If more seeds are released, they
  are analysed and reported, but only seed 1 counts toward the gate.
- Layer: the encoder's first NormedLinear. Its weight E, bias b and LayerNorm ε are
  read from the checkpoint and the code, never assumed.

## Data

- 50 episodes per task: 10 with each environment seed 0–4, with the released agent
  acting as in the repository's `evaluate.py`. Every observation from every step is
  kept.
- Coordinates are exactly what the layer receives, after any preprocessing TD-MPC2
  applies to observations. The preprocessing found is recorded.

## Definitions

All lens quantities follow `docs/theory.md` at the tagged commit.

- μ, C: sample mean and covariance of all observations of a task (numpy.cov
  conventions, ddof = 1).
- Kept dimensions: eigenvectors of C whose eigenvalue is at least 1e-10 times the
  largest. m is their number. C⁺ is the pseudo-inverse of C restricted to them.
- d₁: the unit eigenvector of C with the largest eigenvalue.
- D: project the observations onto d₁, p = (z − μ)·d₁, and set
  D = (q97.5(p) − q2.5(p)) / 2, with numpy's default (linear) percentile method.
- Width along d₁: r_eff(d₁) = r*(d₁)·√(1 + κ) (convention 6). For a degenerate lens
  this is the ε-limited width (convention 4).
- Whitened distance: w(x, y) = √((x − y)ᵀ C⁺ (x − y)).
- Lens distance: ρ(z) = ‖A(z − z*)‖² / ‖c⊥‖² as in docs/theory.md (convention 2),
  defined only for a non-degenerate lens. Effective lens distance:
  ρ_eff(z) = ‖A(z − z*)‖² / (‖c⊥‖² + Hε), which equals ρ/(1 + κ) for a
  non-degenerate lens and stays finite for a degenerate one. Along any line
  through z*, √ρ_eff is the distance in units of r_eff.

## Criterion

A task's lens is "sharp and in the data" if all three hold.

1. **Inside:** w(z*, μ)² is at most the 95% quantile of χ² with m degrees of freedom.
2. **Populated:** draw a subsample of 5,000 observations with
   numpy.random.default_rng(0).choice(n, 5000, replace=False), where n is the
   task's number of observations. For each subsample observation, compute the
   whitened distance to its nearest *other* subsample observation. The whitened
   distance from z* to its nearest subsample observation must be at most the 95th
   percentile of those distances. This rules out a lens centre sitting in an empty
   region inside the data's overall spread.
3. **Sharp:** D / r_eff(d₁) ≥ 5.

## Gate G1 (4 October 2026)

- G1 passes if the criterion holds for at least 2 of the 4 tasks other than
  dog-run. Dog-run is reported but does not count, because its state dimension is
  close to the k ≤ 255 limit of the exact theory.
- If G1 passes, the deployed-model lens becomes the page-1 panel.
- If not, the headline becomes "whether the defect appears depends on a default".

## Reported regardless of outcome

For every task and seed: the preprocessing, E's shape, ε, z*, the principal widths,
κ, whether the lens is degenerate, the three criterion quantities, the
distribution of ρ_eff over the observations (fractions with ρ_eff ≤ 1, 10, 100 and
1,000), the same fractions for ρ when the lens is non-degenerate,
TD-MPC2's initialisation code, and the Corollary 1 identity check on the real layer
along lines through the data.
