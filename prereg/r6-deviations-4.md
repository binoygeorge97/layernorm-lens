## D7. Clarifications fixed before the criterion stage

Each item fixes a choice that the tagged files leave open, or states how a tagged
rule applies under D6. None changes a definition, a threshold or G1.

### (1) D3's tie rule under D6

D3: "If another candidate's e_c is within 10% of identity's (e_c ≤ 1.1·e_identity),
R6 is reported under both. Such a task counts toward gate G1 only if the criterion
holds under both coordinate systems."

This rule stays in force under D6, with symlog in identity's place, for
cartpole-swingup seed 1 and on the planner data (where condition (ii) is binding).
Suppose symlog gives the lowest e among the three D3 candidates. A non-symlog
reading c is within 10% of symlog if e_c / e_symlog ≤ 1.1. If at least one reading
is within 10%, cartpole-swingup seed 1 is ambiguous. In that case it counts as
passing for G1 only if the criterion holds under symlog and under every reading
within 10%. If both non-symlog readings are within 10%, the criterion must hold
under all three; this follows D3's "another candidate". An ambiguous seed 1 remains
the counting checkpoint for cartpole-swingup: it does not hand the vote to seed 2.
Seed 2 votes only if seed 1 is unidentified under D6 (b).

### (2) e₀ in condition (ii)

In D6 (b) condition (ii), e/e₀ ≤ 0.1 uses each candidate's own random-pairing
baseline: e₀ is computed in that candidate's coordinates, with D3's indices
j = numpy.random.default_rng(0).integers(0, n, size=n). This is how
`results/r6/consistency.csv` computes `e_over_e0`, and how D6 quotes symlog's 0.020
for cartpole-swingup seed 1 (e₀ under symlog 38.76; under identity 40.38).

### (3) Populated

r6.md: "Populated: draw a subsample of 5,000 observations with
numpy.random.default_rng(0).choice(n, 5000, replace=False), where n is the task's
number of observations. For each subsample observation, compute the whitened
distance to its nearest *other* subsample observation. The whitened distance from z*
to its nearest subsample observation must be at most the 95th percentile of those
distances."

- Rows: the observations in stored order: environment seed, then episode within
  seed, then step. That is, `obs.reshape(-1, k)` of the stored (50, 501, k) array,
  so n = 25,050. Before using a file, the stage checks that its `env_seed` and
  `episode_in_seed` arrays are in this order, and stops if they are not.
- Subsample: numpy.random.default_rng(0).choice(25050, 5000, replace=False), as
  indices into those rows.
- "Other" means another row index. An exact duplicate at another index counts, at
  distance 0.
- z*'s nearest neighbour is taken from the same 5,000 subsample observations, as
  r6.md says, not from all n observations.
- Whitened distances use C⁺ from all n observations (r6.md, Definitions).
- The 95th percentile is numpy's default (linear) method.

### (4) The Corollary 1 identity on the real layer

r6.md reports "the Corollary 1 identity check on the real layer along lines through
the data". It is checked along these lines, in addition to D6 (g)'s lines through z*:

- 2 lines through μ: one along d₁, one along d₂ (d₂ as in D6 (g); skipped if
  degenerate there);
- 10 lines along d₁, one through each of 10 data states. The states are the rows
  numpy.random.default_rng(0).choice(n, 10, replace=False), in the row order of (3).

Each line x₀ + s·d uses its own closest approach s*, c⊥,ℓ, κ_ℓ and r*_ℓ (theory.md,
Corollary 1), not the lens's. The grid follows D6 (g)'s rule, about the line's own
closest approach x₀ + s*·d: 2,001 points uniform in θ over |θ| ≤ arctan(T / r*_ℓ),
with T = max(max_i |⟨x_i − (x₀ + s*·d), d⟩|, 3·r_eff,ℓ).

At each grid point, the deviation is ‖ĥ − ĥ_Cor1‖₂ / ‖ĥ_Cor1‖₂. Here ĥ is the
layer-1 LayerNorm output before γ and β, computed in float64 from the checkpoint's E
and b, and ĥ_Cor1 is Corollary 1's closed form. The check passes if the maximum
deviation over the grid is at most 1e-10. The maximum deviation is reported for
every line. If any line, here or in D6 (g), exceeds 1e-10, the stage stops before
writing any criterion result, and the author is consulted.

These lines are in the primary coordinates: symlog for the two pre-release
checkpoints. The meta also records tdmpc2's initialisation code
(`tdmpc2/common/init.py` at tdmpc2 commit e9f59321): its path, commit and SHA-256.

### (5) Readings of the pre-release checkpoints

For cartpole-swingup seed 1 and humanoid-run seed 3, Inside, Populated and Sharp are
computed under all three D3 readings of encoder position 0:

- identity: o;
- symlog: sign(o)·log(1 + |o|);
- LayerNorm without affine parameters over the observation vector, ε = 1e-5.

Each reading is applied in float64 to the same stored raw planner observations. The
lens (z*, c⊥, κ, widths) comes from the weights, in the coordinates the layer
receives, so it is the same under every reading. Only the data's coordinates change.

Symlog is the primary reading (D6 (b)); the other two are reported (D5). For
cartpole-swingup seed 1, the non-symlog readings enter G1 only through (1).
humanoid-run seed 3 remains reported only. D6 (g) stays in symlog coordinates for
both checkpoints, as D6 (g) specifies.

### (6) States used for the medians in D6 (g)

The recorded-action median in D6 (g) uses the 25,000 states that have an executed
action: observation t of each episode, paired with the action executed from it,
t = 0, …, 499. Each episode's final observation (t = 500) has no action. Every other
D6 (g) quantity over data states uses all 25,050 states. That includes both
outputs' a = 0 medians and the data range T of the grid.

### (7) Signs of eigenvectors and singular vectors

Eigenvectors of C (d₁, d₂) and singular vectors of A (u_min and the principal
directions) are defined up to sign. Each is signed so that its largest-magnitude
component is positive. A tie in magnitude is broken by the lowest index. This
affects only signed outputs, such as the t of D6 (g)'s peak. No criterion quantity
depends on the sign.

### (8) Computing environment and borderline values

The criterion stage runs on the author's Windows laptop, in float64. Its package
versions are recorded in the stage's meta.

A statistic is borderline if it lies within 1% of its threshold:
|s − τ| ≤ 0.01·|τ|. The statistics are:

- Inside: w(z*, μ)² against the χ²_m 95% quantile;
- Populated: the whitened distance from z* against the 95th percentile;
- Sharp: D / r_eff(d₁) against 5;
- D6 (b) condition (ii), for cartpole-swingup seed 1 on the planner data: symlog's
  e/e₀ against 0.1;
- (1)'s ratio e_c / e_symlog against 1.1, for cartpole-swingup seed 1 on the planner
  data, for each non-symlog reading c.

If any statistic is borderline for any checkpoint and reading, the whole stage is
also run in the Colab CPU environment recorded in
`results/r6/meta_d4_regeneration.json`. That environment is Python 3.13.15, jax and
jaxlib 0.10.2, numpy 2.4.6, scipy 1.17.1, torch 2.14.0+cpu, mujoco 3.14.0,
dm_control 1.0.47 and PyYAML 6.0.1. Both results are reported. If the two disagree
on the side of the threshold:

- for a criterion, that criterion counts as failed for that checkpoint and reading;
- for condition (ii), condition (ii) counts as failed;
- for (1)'s ratio, reading c counts as within 10%.
